# Lena Apt — design system

Modern, minimal Mediterranean boutique: warm neutrals, large photography, one action color, quiet motion. Everything below is implemented; read this before re-reading components.

## Tokens (`src/app/globals.css`)
- **Colors:** `cream #faf6ee` (base) · `cream-deep #f1e9da` ("sand", the alternate section tone) · `cream-line #e4d8c3` (hairlines) · `ink #2b2622` · `ink-soft #5c5448` · `ink-faint #6f6558` · `terracotta #b3542a` (buttons; white text 5.0:1) · `terracotta-dark #93421e` (terracotta *text*, 6.4:1 on cream) · `aegean` (info only) · `olive` (calendar "available"/success only) · `gold #c9a15a` (accents on dark photos).
- **Fonts:** display = **EB Garamond** (`--font-garamond`; has Greek — Playfair did not, so Greek headings used a fallback), body = Inter. Loaded in `src/app/[locale]/layout.tsx`.
- **Type scale (use these, never raw sizes on pages):** `t-eyebrow`, `t-display` (home hero), `t-h1` (page title), `t-h2` (section), `t-h3`, `t-lead`. Body copy is `text-base`/`t-lead` in `ink-soft`.
- **Root size is the global dial:** `html` 15px, 14px from `lg` (the approved "smaller" look). Tailwind is rem-based, so it scales everything. Inputs are forced to 16px (iOS zoom).
- **Rhythm:** `py-section` utility (4.5 → 5.5 → 7rem). Container max `96rem`, gutters `px-5 sm:px-8 lg:px-12` (`ui/Container`). Radius: `rounded-card` (1.25rem) for cards/images, `rounded-full` for buttons/pills. Shadow: `shadow-soft` (one shadow).
- **Section tones alternate** via `Section background="cream" | "deep"`; never two equal tones in a row. Dark blocks: hero photo, `BookingCta` photo only. Footer is light sand.

## Components
- `ui/Button` (+ `buttonStyles()` for real `<button>`s), `ui/Card`, `ui/Notice`, `ui/Section`, `ui/SectionHeading` (eyebrow → title → lead), `ui/Reveal` (fade + 12px rise once, off for reduced motion), `ui/PageHero` (compact | collage), `media/Photo`, `media/PropertyImage`.
- Shell: `layout/Header` (fixed; transparent + white logo over photo heroes on `/` and `/apartment`, solid cream elsewhere/after scroll), `MobileMenu`, `Footer`, `MobileBookBar` (phones, hidden on `/booking`).
- Home (layout follows the owner's mockup): `Hero` (shorter photo, title block centered, filled "Explore" + outline "Book" buttons) → `FactsBar` (on desktop, Hero + FactsBar are wrapped in `lg:min-h-svh` in `page.tsx` so together they fill exactly the first screen; icon strip: bedrooms/beds/bathroom/guests/Wi-Fi/A-C from `config/property`) → `IntroSection` (edge-to-edge split: photo left ~57%, text on cream right) → `ApartmentOverview` (a centered section title "Our apartment / Το διαμέρισμά μας" (`home.overviewTitle`) flanked by soft hairlines that fade out to the sides (`h-px` gradients, `ink/30`), with a compact gap above (pt-8…lg:pt-11) and below it; then only the "View gallery" button, centered, in a narrow left column — grid `0.5fr / 2.5fr` so the photos get most of the width; the row height `lg:h-[26rem]` keeps the photos landscape-ish; the tallest hover text box (bedrooms) must stay below it — no visible title/lead, sr-only heading; 4 photos right via client `SpacesShowcase` (idle: 3 columns — bedrooms | living & kitchen | balconies stacked over bathroom, each stacked photo half the column height; the grouping is `columns` in `ApartmentOverview`). On desktop (lg+) every photo and text panel is absolutely positioned in % of the row (row `lg:h-[26rem]`) by `computeLayout()`, so photos glide with a 0.35s easeInOutSine transition on left/top/width/height. Hovering/focusing bedrooms or living keeps that photo at 33.333% with a 28.667% text panel on its right (white box, 2px terracotta border on top/right/bottom only, rounded right corners, open and square on the photo side so it reads as an extension) and narrows the other columns to 19%; hovering balconies or bathroom breaks the stack: all 4 photos go side by side at full height in reading order (bedrooms, living, balconies, bathroom), the hovered one keeps 33.333% with its panel and the other three narrow to 12.667% (so the bathroom goes to the RIGHT of the balconies when those open, and the balconies go to the left of the bathroom). While a photo is open, another opens only on real pointer movement (not within 350ms of the last switch), so a photo sliding under a motionless cursor (e.g. the bathroom while the balconies open) never steals the open state; entering from outside and keyboard focus open immediately. Durations use Tailwind's trailing `!` to beat the global prefers-reduced-motion rule in `globals.css` (would otherwise make it instant); the text panel is a clipping window against the photo's right edge that widens from 0 to 28.667% with exactly the photo's own transition (left + width, same 0.35s curve), so the box is wiped out of the photo as the row slides — no separate fade; its inner box has a container-unit width so it is revealed, never squeezed; room text reuses `apartment.rooms.*`; below lg each photo is a plain card with its text underneath) → `LocationTeaser` (one cream band split in two: left `ReviewsPanel` — centered in its half but with only its orange "Reviews" eyebrow pinned to the far left as visible text (the title/lead are gone; sr-only heading kept); the band is full-bleed (`max-w-none`, 56px side gutters on 2xl) so there is no empty margin left of the reviews / right of the map on wide screens: 3 platform rating tiles (Google/Booking/Airbnb, each with its brand logo mark + brand colour as a 3px top accent — `reviews/PlatformLogo.tsx`, paths/colours from the `simple-icons` package; the score is drawn the way each platform draws it in `PlatformTile`: Google = number + five yellow stars filled to the score, Booking.com = number in a blue badge with one square corner, Airbnb = solid dark star + number; no "/ 5" shown, an sr-only "/ scale" is kept), 1–2 featured quotes, "read all" button — right the location: title/lead (`home.locationTitle/Lead`, no eyebrow) with the "Discover the location" button on the left and the 4:3 map to their right, vertically centered; the two halves are separated by a 2px `ink/25` vertical rule; the reviews data lives in `config/reviews.ts`, empty fields show a "coming soon" placeholder and the button stays disabled until `allReviewsUrl` is set; the nearby list was removed at the owner's request) → `BookingCta` (roomy photo band, text left / button right; shared with Apartment and Location). Photos live in `config/gallery.ts → homePhotos`. `AmenitiesTeaser` and `property/FactsLine` are no longer on Home (unused).
- Apartment: `PageHero collage` (8 photos, order commented in `apartment/page.tsx`), `StickySubNav`, `PinnedAbout` (desktop pinned scroll — text moves 1:1 with scroll; intentionally *not* disabled by prefers-reduced-motion), `RoomShowcase`, `AmenitiesGrid`.
- Booking: `StepsStrip`, `AvailabilityCalendar` (closed by default, 2 months wide), `BookingDatesContext` (calendar ⇄ form), `BookingForm`, `ContactLinks`.
- Gallery: `GalleryGrid` (underline tabs + CSS-columns masonry using each photo's real `ratio` from `config/gallery.ts`), `Lightbox` (object-contain, keys, swipe, focus trap).
- Explore (`/explore`, nav "Explore / Εξερεύνηση"; replaces the old Location page — `/location` and `/el/location` 308-redirect to `/explore#location` in `next.config.ts`): compact `PageHero` → `StickySubNav` (Location · Nearby · Reviews, reused from Apartment) → `#location` map + address card → `#nearby` (`NearbyPlaces`, sand) → `#reviews` (3 `PlatformTile`s + all `ReviewCard`s or a `ReviewsPlaceholder`) → `BookingCta`. Reviews data and instructions: `config/reviews.ts` (`reviews`, `reviewPlatforms`, `getFeaturedReviews()`); shared components in `components/reviews/`; messages in top-level `reviews` and `explore` namespaces. The home "reviews + location" band links to `/explore#reviews` and `/explore#location`.
- Location: `LocationMap` (+ address `Card` overlapping on desktop; its `OsmEmbed` client child measures the frame and builds the OSM `bbox` from the real aspect ratio, reloading on resize — the raw embed fits its bbox only once and left mis-zoomed, off-centre maps), `NearbyPlaces` (empty categories are hidden until filled in `config/location.ts`).

## Rules of thumb
- **The home page is one cream background** (owner request): no sand/`cream-deep` bands on Home (hero photo and the `BookingCta` photo band excepted). Other pages still alternate cream/sand; the footer stays sand.
- New text goes in `messages/en.json` **and** `messages/el.json`.
- Photo heroes need the transparent header: add the path to `PHOTO_HERO_PATHS` in `Header.tsx`.
- Unused leftovers: `components/home/GalleryTeaser.tsx` (removed from Home at the owner's request; not restyled), `AmenitiesTeaser.tsx`, `property/FactsLine.tsx`.
- The mockup's placeholder facts ("Beach – nearby", "Old Town – short walk", phone/email in the footer) were deliberately NOT copied: nothing is invented (see PRODUCT.md). `home.atAGlance` message is now unused.
